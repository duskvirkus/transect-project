● Here are three:

  ---
  1. Delete unused Vite scaffold assets

  src/assets/react.svg and src/assets/vite.svg are the default npm create vite
  template files. Nothing in the project imports them. Pure dead weight.

  ---
  2. Merge App.css into index.css

  There are two global CSS files with no meaningful distinction between them —
  index.css has global/reset styles and App.css has app-shell styles, but
  they're both flat global scope. Splitting them just means you have to look in
  two places when hunting for a style rule. One CSS file is simpler.

  ---
  3. Organize src/ into subdirectories

  All 10+ JSX files are flat in src/. A minimal split:

  src/
    pages/        ← LandingPage, BuilderPage, CollectDataPage,
                     AnalysisPage, CreateATransectPage, WhatIsFelsiusPage
    components/   ← NavHeader, TempScaleToggle, TempScaleContext
    lib/          ← temperature.js, transect.js, climate.js (and their tests)
    data/         (already gone)

  Most invasive of the three since it touches every import, but it's the kind of
   thing that becomes a bigger lift the longer you defer it. This project has 6
  pages and will likely add more for Part III.
